# Code of conduct

Welcome contributors regardless of background, identity, experience or ability. Be respectful and keep criticism focused on the work. Provide constructive, actionable feedback.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, private-information disclosure, sexualized attention and sustained disruption are unacceptable in project spaces. Report concerns privately to `hello@mattiapiazzalunga.com`.

Maintainers handle reports discreetly and apply proportionate clarification, warnings, content removal, temporary restrictions or exclusion. Retaliation is unacceptable. Maintainers follow the same standards and disclose conflicts when handling reports.
